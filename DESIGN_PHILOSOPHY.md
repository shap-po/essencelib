# EssenceLib: Mod Design Philosophy & Game Design Document (GDD)

## 1. Core Inspiration
This mod is heavily inspired by the hardcore progression and brutal world of **"Surviving the Game as a Barbarian"**. 
The core philosophy revolves around the harsh, unforgiving nature of survival where every choice matters. Abilities, stats, and progression should reflect the struggle to survive and adapt. Power isn't free; it comes with sacrifices, risks, and a deep understanding of your own build.

## 2. The Philosophy (Falso-fy) of the Mod
* **High Risk, High Reward:** Surviving means making tough choices. No essence is perfectly good without a catch.
* **Adaptation:** You must fuse with the essences of the monsters you defeat to survive. Your playstyle must adapt to the strengths and weaknesses of the essences you absorb.
* **The Value of Knowledge:** Just like in the book, knowing which mobs drop which essences and how to combine them is the key to thriving.

## 3. The Essence System
When a player absorbs an essence, they inherit a portion of that monster's soul and abilities. 

### Structure
Each Essence grants exactly:
* **1 Active Ability:** A triggered skill or power (e.g., a burst of speed, a specific attack, a defensive shield).
* **1 Passive Ability:** An always-on effect (e.g., fire resistance, slow fall, increased knockback).

### Stat Balance (The Give and Take)
* **Always Positive and Negative:** Every essence will alter your base stats (Health, Speed, Armor, Attack Damage, etc.). However, **every essence must have both positive and negative stat modifiers**. (e.g., +20% Attack Damage, but -10% Movement Speed).
* **Rarity:** Essences are divided by rarity. Rarer essences provide more drastic changes or unique abilities, but finding them requires hunting specific, dangerous foes or bosses.
* **Build Diversity:** The stats are balanced to allow for two main types of play:
    * **Optimal Builds:** For hardcore players who want to min-max their stats for pure survival and efficiency.
    * **Wacky/Fun Builds:** For players who want to combine bizarre essences for unexpected, chaotic, or highly specialized playstyles (e.g., a glass cannon leaping build).

## 4. "Lifestyles" Mechanic
To bridge the gap between the book's combat-heavy focus and Minecraft's sandbox nature, the mod introduces **Lifestyles**.
* A player's "Lifestyle" represents their core Minecraft playstyle (e.g., Miner, Farmer, Nomad, Builder, Slayer).
* **Fusion:** The player's chosen Lifestyle fuses with the essences they absorb, changing how those essences manifest. 
* *Example:* A "Miner" lifestyle might slightly alter a Zombie essence to provide better vision in the dark, while a "Slayer" lifestyle might alter the same essence to provide more raw damage.

## 5. The Gameplay Loop (Grind & Economy)
The core loop of the mod focuses on hunting, collecting, and trading.

1. **Targeted Grinding:** Players will need to hunt down specific mobs to get the exact essences they need for their desired build.
2. **Caging & Capturing:** Players will have a way to capture or cage physical essences.
3. **The Economy:** Because finding the right essence is hard, an economy emerges. Players can sell, trade, or buy caged essences with other players on servers.
